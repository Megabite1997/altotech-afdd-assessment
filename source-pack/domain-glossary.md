# Domain Glossary

## Property or building

A managed physical building. This assessment contains two office buildings and one hotel.

## Floor, HVAC zone, and room

A building contains floors and rooms. An HVAC zone groups rooms that receive conditioned air together. In this assessment, each zone contains two occupied rooms.

## Plant room

A non-occupied technical room where the AHUs in this simplified model are physically installed. An AHU's installed location is different from the occupied spaces it serves.

## Air handling unit

An air handling unit, or AHU, moves and conditions air for a served HVAC zone.

## Served space

The HVAC zone that receives air from an AHU. The rooms inside that zone are potentially affected when the AHU is not operating correctly.

## Telemetry point

One measurable or status-producing property of a device or machine, such as supply-air temperature or run status.

## Supply-air temperature

The measured temperature of air leaving an AHU.

## Supply-air temperature setpoint

The target supply-air temperature that the AHU is expected to maintain.

## Return-air temperature

The temperature of air returning from the served spaces to the AHU.

## Indoor air quality sensor

An IAQ sensor measures conditions inside an occupied room. This assessment provides room temperature, relative humidity, and carbon dioxide concentration.

## Electricity meter

A device that measures active electrical power in kilowatts and cumulative electrical energy in kilowatt-hours. Each supplied meter represents one floor.

## Automatic Fault Detection and Diagnostics

Automatic Fault Detection and Diagnostics, or AFDD, evaluates operating conditions and recent telemetry. An AFDD issue is a reason to investigate. It is not proof of a mechanical root cause.

## Brickschema

An open ontology for describing building spaces, equipment, telemetry points, and their relationships. Brickschema defines shared meaning; it does not require a particular database product.
